Cat-Activated Fireplace

The cat settles into its bed on the hearth, and the fireplace comes on.

How it works

  1. An Ideal Security pressure mat sits under the cat bed.
  2. An Aqara Zigbee leak sensor, converted to work as a contact sensor, is connected to the mat and reports when the cat is lying on it.
  3. A Homey Pro hub notices the change and switches Zooz ZEN17 relays.
  4. The relays light the Heat & Glo fireplace.

More details to come. This describes one home's setup; it is not installation instructions, and gas fireplaces must be wired and used safely.
