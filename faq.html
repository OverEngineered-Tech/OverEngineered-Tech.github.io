FAQ

Questions about the OverEngineered Solar widgets.

Is it free?

Yes, for personal and other noncommercial use, under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0.

Is it real-time?

No. It updates about once an hour while the sun is up, because Enphase's free plan allows about 1,000 requests a month.

Do I need a computer?

No. It runs on your iPhone in the Scriptable app. A computer is only handy for reading the printable guide or setting up the Enphase developer account.

Where does my data go?

Your settings and keys stay on your phone. The widget talks only to Enphase (your solar data), Open-Meteo (weather) and Zippopotam.us (ZIP code lookup).

What do I need?

An iPhone with Scriptable, an Enphase system with the login you use in the Enphase app, and a free Enphase developer account. The setup script walks you through the last one.

Does it show battery status?

No.

Is this affiliated with Enphase?

No. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Enphase Energy, Inc. Enphase is a registered trademark of Enphase Energy, Inc.
